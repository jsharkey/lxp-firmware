## Where the data comes from

**The battery BMS, first-hand over CAN1.** The ARM is the inverter-side CAN
driver; it decodes the pack's broadcast frames and maps them into this register
block. No DSP or safety-CPU involvement. See
[`docs/20`](../20-battery-bms-firmware.md) (the pack-side frame contract + the
CAN→IR map) and [`docs/wiring/ARM-CAN1`](../wiring/ARM-CAN1.md) (the ARM-side
driver: RX ring `0x200051ac`, parser `sub_0802a760`).

Representative CAN-frame → register mappings (from the factory oracle,
[`docs/20`](../20-battery-bms-firmware.md)):

| CAN ID | carries | → registers |
|---|---|---|
| `0x351` | charge/discharge V & I limits | 83 (ChargeVoltRef), 81/82 (currents), 84 |
| `0x355` | SOC / SOH | IR 5 (see [`IR005-SOC_SOH`](IR005-SOC_SOH.md)) |
| `0x356` | battery V / I / T | 98 (current), temps |
| `0x359` | protection / alarm bits | 99/100 (fault/warn), 96 |

The committed contract is [`include/inv/bms_can_map.h`](../../include/inv/bms_can_map.h).

## How BatStatus0-9 (IR 85-94) are generated

The "BMS status words" label is only partly accurate on the `ceaa-07xx` image:
of the ten slots, **only three (87/88/89) actually come from the battery BMS**,
five (90-94) come from the **safety CPU**, and two (85/86) are inactive. Recovered
by injecting each BMS CAN frame through the harness and reading 85-94 back over
the dongle (`tests/arm_sim_can.py` + `arm_sim_modbus.py`):

| IR | name | source | detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 85 | BatStatus0 | — | **inactive** (reads `0`); no CAN or USART2 feed on this image |
| 86 | BatStatus1 | — | **inactive** (reads `0`) |
| 87 | BatStatus2 | CAN `0x35C` | request flags: **high byte** = raw `0x35C` byte0 (bit3 cleared); **low byte** = inverter-summary flags (bit4/5→`0x10`, bit6→`0x02`, bit7→`0x01`) — the low byte equals **IR 95** `BatStatus_INV` |
| 88 | BatStatus3 | CAN `0x359` [0:1] | protection bitfield — **identical to IR 99** `FaultCode_BMS` |
| 89 | BatStatus4 | CAN `0x359` [2:3] | alarm bitfield — **identical to IR 100** `WarningCode_BMS` |
| 90-91 | BatStatus5/6 | USART2 bank `+0xbc` | **safety CPU**, not BMS — the BE u32 at `BANK+0xbc`, split (docs/33) |
| 92-93 | BatStatus7/8 | USART2 bank `+0xb8` | safety CPU — BE u32 at `BANK+0xb8`, split (docs/33) |
| 94 | BatStatus9 | USART2 bank `+0xc4` | safety CPU — BE u16 at `BANK+0xc4` (docs/33) |

So the only *battery-sourced* status words are the `0x35C` request-flags word
(87) and the two `0x359` protection/alarm words (88/89) — and 88/89 are plain
duplicates of the fault/warn registers IR 99/100. Everything 90-94 is the
safety-CPU telemetry misfiled under the BMS-status label (see the variant caveat
below and [`docs/33`](../33-usart2-telemetry-bank.md)); 85/86 never populate.
Reproduce: inject `0x359`/`0x35C` and read 85-100 — e.g. `0x359` fault `0x0102` /
warn `0x0408` → IR 88=IR 99=`0x0102`, IR 89=IR 100=`0x0408`; `0x35C` byte0=`0x80`
→ IR 87=`0x8001`, IR 95=`0x0001`.

## Nuances

- **Signed:** IR 98 (current), IR 103/104 (cell temps) are `s16`.
- **Charge/discharge switches:** the dongle can toggle them via `HR 11` bits 5/6
  ([`HR011-ResetSetting`](HR011-ResetSetting.md)), which the ARM then
  commands to the pack over CAN1.
- **Not to be confused with** the inverter's own fault/warn words (IR 60-63) — the
  BMS has its own at IR 99/100.
- **Variant/map caveat:** the ceaa-07xx factory getter serves IR 90-94 from the
  USART2 bank (`BANK+0xb8/0xbc`) rather than as BMS-status, disagreeing with the
  18kPV superset label for those four — flagged in
  [`docs/33`](../33-usart2-telemetry-bank.md). Verify IR 90-94 before relying on
  them for this image.
